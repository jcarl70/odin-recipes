Cotoletta recipe

Recipe description

A Cotoletta alla Milanese is a breaded veal (or other cutlet) that’s fried until golden and crispy, traditionally in clarified butter. Use a bone-in, thick rib chop, coat it evenly with breadcrumbs, then fry in clarified butter for the classic texture and flavor.

Steps to Prepare Cotoletta

  1. Prepare the Cutlets Trim any excess fat from the veal cutlets. Use a meat tenderizer to even out the thickness without pounding them too thin.
  2. Coat the Cutlets Dredge each cutlet in breadcrumbs, ensuring an even coating
  3. Fry the Cutlets Heat clarified butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the breaded cutlets and fry until golden brown on both sides. Drain on paper towels to remove excess fat.
  4. Serve Sprinkle with salt and serve warm, optionally with a wedge of lemon for added flavor.
  5. Tips for Success Clarified Butter: This has a higher smoke point than regular butter, making it ideal for frying. Thickness: Ensure cutlets are no thicker than 3 cm for even cooking. Serving Suggestions: Cotoletta is often enjoyed with a side of lemon wedges and can be paired with a fresh salad or potatoes.

Ingridients

  • Veal cutlets (bone-in) 4 pieces, 3 cm thick
  • Clarified butter 300 g
  • Breadcrumbs As needed
  • Salt To taste
