Lasagna recipe

Recipe description

This lasagna recipe will make you want more.

Steps to Prepare Simple Lasagna

  1. Prepare the Meat Sauce In a skillet, brown the ground beef until fully cooked. Drain excess fat and stir in the marinara sauce. Simmer for a few minutes.
    2. Prepare the Meat Sauce
  2. pread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Layer 3-4 lasagna noodles over the sauce. Spread a portion of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles. Add another layer of meat sauce and sprinkle with mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce and mozzarella on top.
  3. Bake the Lasagna Cover the dish with aluminum foil and bake in a preheated oven at 375°F for about 45 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before serving.
  4. Tips for Best Results Use high-quality marinara sauce for better flavor. Allow the lasagna to rest after baking to help it set and make slicing easier. This recipe can be made ahead of time and frozen for later use. Just thaw and bake as directed.

Ingridients

  • Lasagna noodles, 12 ounces (no-boil)
  • Ground beef, 1 pound
  • Marinara sauce, 24 to 26 ounces
  • Ricotta cheese, 15 ounces
  • Mozzarella cheese, 2 cups, shredded
  • Water, 1 cup
