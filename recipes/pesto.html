Pesto recipe

Recipe description

Traditional basil pesto is made from fresh basil, garlic, pine nuts, extra-virgin olive oil, and Parmesan cheese, blended together in a food processor. It takes about 10 minutes to make, and you can use it on pasta, salads, roasted veggies, or sandwiches..

Steps to Prepare Pesto

  1. Prepare Ingredients: Gather all ingredients and a food processor.
  2. Blend Nuts and Garlic: Pulse the pine nuts, garlic, and a pinch of salt in the food processor until finely chopped.
  3. Add Basil: Add the fresh basil leaves and pulse again until mixed.
  4. Incorporate Olive Oil: With the processor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until the mixture is smooth.
  5. Mix in Parmesan: Add the grated Parmesan cheese and pulse briefly to combine.
  6. Taste and Adjust: Taste the pesto and adjust seasoning with more salt if needed.

Ingridients

  • Fresh basil 2 cups (packed)
  • Garlic 1-2 cloves
  • Pine nuts ½ cup
  • Extra-virgin olive oil ½ cup
  • Parmesan cheese ½ cup (grated)
  • Salt To taste
